B.Tech Roadmap

Follow your journey from first year to professional engineer.

Year 1 — Foundation

Learn programming fundamentals, engineering mathematics, basic electronics, communication skills and computer basics.

Year 2 — Core Skills

Master data structures, databases, operating systems, computer networks and object-oriented programming.

Year 3 — Specialization

Choose your specialization, build advanced projects, attend hackathons and search for internships.

Year 4 — Career

Prepare your resume, practice coding interviews, complete your final-year project and prepare for placements.
